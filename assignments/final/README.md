# Final Project

## Grade

- Prototype(s) are worth 25% of final grade
- Presentation is worth 7% of final grade

## Deadlines

- Presentations will be held in class during the final week (week of 30 December 2026)
- Prototype(s) are due one week *after* the final class (week of 7 December 2026)

🔥 If you are running into trouble, get in touch with your instructor *early* to work out a plan, we want to help
🔥 Read the submission guidelines carefully. You will lose points for incorrect submission formats.

## Objectives

- Demonstrating your mastery of the diverse technical concepts and skills learned over the course of the semester
- Exploring the different potentials and challenges of a larger scale project
- Reflecting on your path through programming both as a technical and artistic/design practice during this course

## Deliverables

See the more specific pages for details:

- One larger prototype or a coherent collection of smaller prototypes along with weekly journal entries reflecting on your progress, see: [Final Prototype: Prototype](./prototype/)
